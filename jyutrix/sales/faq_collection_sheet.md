# Approved administrative answers

For each clinic, collect question, approved answer, approving owner and review date.

- Timings, breaks, holidays and leave.
- Address, directions and clinic phone.
- Consultation fee; treatment prices only if expressly approved.
- Services offered without diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Payment methods.
- Booking/cancellation policy.
- Staff contact and coverage.

Avoid copying medical advice or generic general-clinic services into a dental clinic. The application can semantically select an approved FAQ when OpenAI is configured, but cannot independently verify the clinic's claims. Owners must review answers.
