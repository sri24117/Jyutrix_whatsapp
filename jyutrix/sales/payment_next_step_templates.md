# Pilot follow-up drafts

## After a demo

Thank you for reviewing Jyutrix. The proposed 30-day pilot covers one clinic, one doctor and one WhatsApp number: approved enquiry replies, confirmed bookings, reminders and staff handoff. Proposed pilot price is ₹4,999 including basic setup; renewal is ₹4,999/month with the agreed message allowance and support hours. We will activate after number/template setup and a supervised acceptance test. Shall we agree a start date and the staff member who will maintain the schedule?

## Weekly review

This week's review covers enquiries, bookings, attended visits, no-shows and staff actions. These are operational counts, not a claim of extra revenue caused by the software. Please confirm which changes were useful and any missed or incorrect messages.

## Renewal discussion

Your pilot ends on [date]. Would you like to renew at the agreed monthly price and allowance? Before renewal, let's review attendance, reception workload, message failures and the support needed to keep the workflow reliable.
