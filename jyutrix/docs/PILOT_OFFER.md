# First paid clinic pilot

## Offer to test

30-day managed pilot for one dental clinic, one doctor, one WhatsApp number and one schedule. Proposed pilot price: ₹4,999 including basic setup; proposed renewal ₹4,999/month. These are price experiments, not market-validated rates. Agree a 2,000 outgoing-message allowance, usage charges, taxes as applicable and support hours before payment. Voice is excluded from this offer.

Includes approved FAQs, confirmed appointment coordination, reminders, staff handoff and a weekly results review. No promises of replacing staff, increasing revenue by a fixed amount or giving medical advice.

## Discovery

Ask the owner about weekly WhatsApp volume, delayed enquiries, after-hours responses, no-shows, current scheduling and who maintains reception. Request anonymized examples. Qualify clinics with existing enquiry volume and a receptionist willing to maintain one shared schedule.

## Onboarding

Collect approved name/address/fees/services, doctor schedule, breaks/leave, escalation phone, WhatsApp ownership, staff accounts, template permission, data handling requirements and pilot acceptance criteria. Run LIVE_ACCEPTANCE.md with the clinic owner. Train reception on takeover, manual bookings, attendance, payment recording and failed-send review.

## Weekly review

Measure enquiries, confirmed bookings, attended visits, no-shows, failed messages and staff minutes spent on support. These are operational counts; they do not prove the software caused additional visits. Compare against a pre-pilot baseline and discuss specific missed enquiries where possible.

Track your own direct messaging/AI/hosting costs and support hours. Renew only where the clinic sees useful value and delivery leaves a margin after your time. Three paying clinics and two renewals are proposed validation targets, not established results.

## Demo sequence

1. Show approved fees/address.
2. Patient asks to book; show offered slots and explicit confirmation.
3. Show the confirmed record in the schedule.
4. Reschedule/cancel with confirmation.
5. Ask for staff; take over in the inbox.
6. Explain reminder permission and delivery tracking.
7. State what the current pilot includes and excludes; ask for a paid start date.

The templates in `sales/` follow this offer. Personalize dates, allowances and terms before using them. No messages have been sent by this build.
