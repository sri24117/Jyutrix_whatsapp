What to expect

A few important boundaries.

Does Jyutrix replace our receptionist?

No. Staff remain responsible for clinic operations and can take over conversations. The clinic must monitor the inbox and enter non-WhatsApp bookings in the Jyutrix schedule.

Does it give medical advice?

No. Jyutrix is for reception workflows, not diagnosis, treatment recommendations or emergency triage. Patients should contact the clinic or local emergency services for urgent help.

Which languages does it support?

Current automated replies are primarily in English. Some Telugu and Hindi expressions are recognized, but complete translated conversations have not been validated. Agree staff coverage and test the clinic’s languages before a pilot.

Does it sync with our existing calendar?

No. The pilot uses a Jyutrix schedule. The clinic team must enter phone and walk-in appointments here to keep availability accurate.
